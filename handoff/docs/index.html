What it is

Labelers draw in OncoStudio (the labeling build). Everything they do reaches the hub, the system of record for projects, assignments, reviews, label revisions, datasets and splits, runs, models, evaluations, releases, training jobs and policies. Training runs on the executor, a headless service the hub drives; it runs the research pipeline at a pinned, verified commit. ai-pipeline computes the regulatory metrics, packages releases and owns the ROI registry file. Nothing here is a research tool: the platform records and automates what the data team already does, in one place.

Principles the platform keeps

  • No patient names anywhere in the hub; patient IDs are masked below the lead role.
  • Additive contracts: an installed OncoStudio keeps working against the newest hub; migrations never drop or rename.
  • The pipeline is pinned: the executor runs one verified commit + image of the research code and never edits it.
  • Any labeled sample may train (v1.4): approved, unreviewed, changes-requested, AI pre-label or legacy — its label state is recorded with the split, and policies may prefer approved data but never refuse the rest.
  • Documentation is part of the change: a behaviour, contract or UI change updates its page in the same commit (a gate refuses otherwise).

Behind the pages

The binding API text is onco-mlops/CONTRACT.md; every non-obvious choice and its reason is in DECISIONS.md (U# user decisions, D# agent decisions); operations in RUNBOOK.md; the role registry in WORKSPACES.md. Those files live in the shared repository on the platform host (/drive/project/onco-mlops) and are snapshotted into every workspace's handoff/.
